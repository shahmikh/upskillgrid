Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what information upskillgrid.com (“UpskillGrid”, “we”) collects and how it is used. If you have questions, email hello@upskillgrid.com.

Information we collect

  • Email you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.
  • Server and security logs. The site is hosted on Cloudflare. Like most hosts, Cloudflare processes technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages requested to deliver and protect the site.

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not sell personal information.

Cookies and advertising

We may use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info. To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where the law requires it, for example in the EEA and the UK, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads or non-essential cookies are used.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which guides are useful. It does not use cookies, does not track you across other websites, and does not build a profile of you. It records basic page-view data such as the page visited, the referring site, browser type, country, and page load speed. Cloudflare’s handling of this data is described in its privacy policy.

Links to other sites

Guides link to official course and product pages. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Children

This site is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to delete any email correspondence with you. Depending on where you live, you may have other rights over your personal data. Contact us to use them.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date at the top.
