5 free Python courses where the certificate is also free

By Syed Shahmikh Ali · Published 7 November 2026 · Updated 7 November 2026

The five best Python courses where both the course and the certificate are free are Harvard's CS50P, freeCodeCamp's Python Certification, the University of Helsinki Python MOOC, Kaggle's Python course and Cisco's Python Essentials 1. Many other "free" Python courses only let you watch for free and then charge for the certificate. Below is what each of these five takes to finish and which one suits your goal.

Key takeaways

  • Harvard CS50P gives a free certificate through Harvard's own CS50 site. The same course on edX charges for its certificate.
  • freeCodeCamp's Python Certification, launched in December 2025, needs 5 projects and a 50-question exam.
  • The University of Helsinki MOOC is the most demanding: you must pass an online exam on a set date.
  • Kaggle (about 5 hours) is the fastest. Cisco Python Essentials 1 prepares you for the paid PCEP exam.
  • A certificate shows you finished a course. Your own projects show you can code, so build at least one.

Which free Python courses give a free certificate?

These five courses are free to study and do not charge for the certificate. They differ a lot in how much work the certificate needs:

If you only want one, start with CS50P. If you want the certificate that proves the most, aim for freeCodeCamp or Helsinki, because both test you instead of only tracking that you finished.

Is Harvard's CS50P certificate really free?

Yes, if you take it the right way. CS50P is Harvard's introduction to programming with Python, taught by David J. Malan. Harvard Online lists it at 10 weeks and 3 to 9 hours a week, and on edX you can audit it for free, but the edX certificate is a paid upgrade, according to the Harvard Online course page.

The free certificate comes from Harvard's own CS50 site, not edX. Class Central's guide to CS50P explains the rule: score at least 70% on every problem set and on the final project, and you get a free certificate of completion.

Why I recommend it to most beginners: the lectures are excellent, and the problem sets make you write real programs from week one. It covers functions, loops, exceptions, libraries, unit tests, file input and output, regular expressions and object-oriented programming.

What does the freeCodeCamp Python certification require?

freeCodeCamp launched its new Python Certification on December 15, 2025. To earn it you complete the curriculum, build five certification projects and pass a timed, closed-book 50-question exam, as described in freeCodeCamp's announcement. Module quizzes have 20 questions and you need 18 correct to pass.

You can take the exam once a week, and staff review passing exams before the result is released. You need a laptop or desktop for the exam. Everything, including the certification, is free.

This is the best choice if you want a certificate that shows you can build working programs, because the projects are graded automatically against tests.

How do you pass the University of Helsinki Python MOOC?

The Helsinki MOOC is a real university course put online. It has 14 parts: parts 1 to 7 are Introduction to Programming and parts 8 to 14 are the Advanced Course in Programming. The 2026 version stays open until the end of 2026.

To pass, you need at least 25% of the exercise points in each part and at least 50% of the exam points, with exercises and exam each counting for half of your grade, according to the course's grading and exams page. The exam is online and lasts four hours. Upcoming Introduction to Programming exam dates are 5 December 2026 and 16 January 2027.

Once you pass, you can download your certificate from your mooc.fi profile. Choose this course if you want depth and do not mind text-based lessons without videos.

Which free Python course is fastest: Kaggle or Cisco?

Kaggle Python is the fastest. It takes about 5 hours, is free, and gives you a certificate of completion from Kaggle, according to Shiksha's course listing. Lessons run in your browser, so you do not need to install anything. It leads straight into Kaggle's free pandas and machine learning courses, so pick it if you are heading toward data science.

Cisco Python Essentials 1 is free, takes about 30 hours and gives you a digital badge from Cisco Networking Academy, as summarised by Opportunities Feed. It is built with the OpenEDG Python Institute to prepare you for the PCEP entry-level Python certification. The course is free, but the PCEP exam itself is paid.

Which free Python course should you choose?

Here is how I would decide, after years of teaching Python one to one:

  • Total beginner who wants one course: Harvard CS50P.
  • Want proof you can build projects: freeCodeCamp Python Certification.
  • Want university-level depth or credits: University of Helsinki MOOC.
  • Heading into data science or AI: Kaggle Python, then Kaggle's pandas course.
  • Want an exam-based Python certification later: Cisco Python Essentials 1, then the PCEP exam.
  • School student in IB or AP Computer Science: CS50P fits the course well. Our free IB points calculator and AP Computer Science score calculator can help you plan your grades.

Are free Python certificates worth it?

A free certificate is worth getting, but it is not what gets you hired. It shows you finished a structured course. What employers and admissions tutors look for is proof that you can solve problems in code.

So use the certificate as a checkpoint, then build one small project of your own: a quiz game, a grade calculator, or a script that renames files or cleans a spreadsheet. Put the code on GitHub and link it next to your certificate. Frequently asked questions

Can I really get a Harvard Python certificate for free?

Yes. Take CS50P through Harvard's own CS50 site and score at least 70% on every problem set and the final project. The edX version of the same course charges for its verified certificate.

Which free Python certificate is the hardest to earn?

The University of Helsinki MOOC, because you must pass a timed online exam on a set date. The freeCodeCamp certification is next, with five projects and a 50-question exam.

How long does it take to learn Python for free?

For the basics, about 5 hours on Kaggle or about 30 hours with Cisco Python Essentials 1. For solid fundamentals, plan on roughly 10 weeks with CS50P at 3 to 9 hours a week.
