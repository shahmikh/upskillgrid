UpskillGrid is run by Syed Shahmikh Ali. I studied mechatronics engineering and now teach computer science one to one. Students ask me the same questions every week: which AI tool should I use, is this course really free, will this certificate help me get a job. UpskillGrid is where I answer those questions properly, once, with sources.
