How is the AP Computer Science A exam scored?

The AP CSA exam has two sections of 1 hour 30 minutes each. Section I is 42 multiple-choice questions worth 55% of your score, and Section II is 4 free-response questions worth 45%, according to the College Board AP CSA exam page. The four free-response questions always follow the same pattern: methods and control structures, class design, data analysis with an ArrayList, and a 2D array.

The free-response questions are not all worth the same. In the 2026 AP CSA scoring guidelines, Question 1 and Question 2 were worth 7 points each, Question 3 was worth 5 points and Question 4 was worth 6 points, so 25 points in total.

How is AP Computer Science Principles scored?

AP CSP has two parts. Section I is 70 multiple-choice questions in 120 minutes, worth 70% of your score. Section II is worth 30% and combines the Create performance task you build during the year with 2 written-response questions answered on exam day, as described on the College Board AP CSP exam page.

Section II is marked on six rows, and each row is worth 0 or 1 point. The 2026 AP CSP scoring guidelines list them as: the video, the program requirements, Written Response 1 on program design, function and purpose, and Written Response 2(a), 2(b) and 2(c) on algorithm development, errors and testing, and data and procedural abstraction. That makes 6 points, scaled to 30.

So 50 multiple-choice answers correct plus 5 of the 6 rows gives 50 + (5 ÷ 6 × 30) = 75, an estimated 4. For 2027, the Create task is due by 11:59 PM ET on April 30, 2027, and the end-of-course exam is on May 14, 2027. College Board says the course is being redesigned for the 2027 to 2028 school year, so later exams may change.

How accurate is this AP score calculator?

The section weights and point totals come straight from College Board, so the composite is exact for the points you enter. The step from composite to a 1 to 5 score is an estimate, because the real cut points move a few points each year.

Two things make your estimate better:

  • Use a full, timed practice test. A score from half a test or untimed practice will flatter you.
  • Mark free responses honestly against the official rubric. College Board notes that free-response questions from before the 2025 to 2026 AP CSA revision do not fully match the current exam, and older CSA questions were usually out of 9 points. If you practise with an older question, convert your points to the current scale before you enter them.

How can you raise your AP Computer Science score?

These are the habits I see make the biggest difference with my AP students:

  • CSA: never leave a free-response question blank. Points are awarded for separate parts of the solution, so a correct method header, a working loop and a correct return statement can earn points even when the full answer is wrong.
  • CSA: practise 2D array traversal until it is automatic. Row by row and column by column loops appear every year.
  • CSA: trace code on paper for multiple choice. Most mistakes come from guessing what a loop does instead of writing down each variable.
  • CSP: treat each Section II row as its own target. Each row is 0 or 1, so read the scoring guidelines and make sure your video, code and written responses clearly meet every row.
  • Both: answer every multiple-choice question. Your score counts correct answers, so a blank and a wrong guess both earn zero.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a penalty for guessing on AP Computer Science exams?

No. Multiple-choice scores count only correct answers, so a wrong guess costs you nothing. Answer every question, even if you have to guess at the end.

What is a good AP Computer Science score?

A 3 is a pass and some colleges give credit for it. Many colleges ask for a 4 or 5 for credit in a computer science course, so check the AP credit policy of each college you are applying to.

Can I use old AP CSA practice questions with this calculator?

Yes, but convert the points first. Free-response questions from before the 2025 to 2026 revision were usually out of 9, while current ones are worth 7, 7, 5 and 6. Work out your percentage on the old question and apply it to the current point value.
