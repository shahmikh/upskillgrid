How to Use ChatGPT Study Mode (and Similar Modes in Gemini and Claude)

By Syed Shahmikh Ali · Published 30 August 2026 · Updated 30 August 2026

ChatGPT study mode is a free setting that makes ChatGPT behave like a tutor. Instead of giving you the answer, it asks what you already know, guides you one step at a time and checks your understanding. To turn it on, open the plus menu in a chat and choose Study, or type @study. It works on every plan, including free. Gemini and Claude have similar modes called Guided Learning and learning mode.

Key takeaways

  • Study mode is free on all ChatGPT plans and needs no separate app.
  • It guides you with questions and hints instead of full answers.
  • You can switch it off at any time, so it only helps if you choose to stay in it.
  • Gemini's Guided Learning and Claude's learning mode work in a similar way.

What is ChatGPT study mode?

OpenAI launched study mode in July 2025 to answer a fair criticism: students were using ChatGPT to get homework answers without learning anything. Implicator's report on the launch explains that study mode is free for all ChatGPT users, including the free tier, and that it was aimed first at college students but works for high school students too. Users must be 13 or older, with parental consent for ages 13 to 17.

Pearson's guide for international schools describes the idea well: these tools are designed to slow students down and guide them through the learning process step by step, focusing on learning, not output.

How do you turn study mode on?

Echai's walkthrough lists three ways: type @study in the message box, pick Study from the plus menu, or open chatgpt.com/studymode. It works on any plan, including free, on phones and in a browser.

  1. Sign in to ChatGPT. A free account is enough.
  2. Start a new chat and switch study mode on using one of the methods above.
  3. Say what you are studying and at what level, for example 'IGCSE Computer Science, binary and hexadecimal'.
  4. Upload your notes, a worksheet or a past paper question if you have one.
  5. Answer its questions honestly. If you do not know, say so, and it will back up a step.

What should you ask in study mode?

Study mode works best when you give it a clear goal. These prompts work well in my own lessons:

  • 'I have a test on recursion on Friday. Find out what I already know, then teach me the gaps.'
  • 'Here is a past paper question. Do not solve it. Ask me questions until I can.'
  • 'Quiz me on these notes, one question at a time, and make the questions harder when I get them right.'
  • 'I think the answer is 42. Do not tell me if I am right. Ask me to explain my working.'
  • 'Explain this to me as if I were 12, then ask me to explain it back.'

The last one uses a proven technique: explaining an idea in your own words shows you exactly where your understanding stops. For more patterns, read prompt writing basics.

How do Gemini and Claude compare?

All three major assistants now have a tutoring mode. Glasp's comparison of AI study modes notes that ChatGPT Study Mode launched in July 2025, Gemini Guided Learning in August 2025 and Claude's learning mode in April 2025, and that each uses guided questioning instead of direct answers.

ChatGPT Study ModeGemini Guided LearningClaude learning mode
StyleConversational tutorMore visual, with diagrams, videos and quizzesShort questions that make you reason
Free accessYes, on every planYes, with usage limitsCheck your plan or university account
Good forAny subject, quick practiceVisual topics and revisionEssays, code and documents

Glasp also reports that Google's free year of AI Pro for students ended in March 2026, so heavy Gemini users may hit free limits sooner. For a full look at what each free plan includes, see ChatGPT vs Gemini vs Claude free plans.

What are the limits of study mode?

  • It is optional. Implicator points out that teachers and parents cannot lock a student into study mode. One click returns you to normal answers, so the discipline is yours.
  • It can be wrong. It may accept a flawed explanation or teach a method your exam board does not use. Check against your textbook and mark schemes.
  • It does not know your syllabus unless you tell it. Name the exam board and paste the relevant specification points.
  • It is not a substitute for practice papers. Use it to understand, then test yourself under timed conditions.

If you want answers grounded only in your own notes, pair study mode with NotebookLM; see how to use NotebookLM for studying.

What is a good study routine with it?

  1. Read the topic in your notes first, for ten minutes, without AI.
  2. Open study mode and ask it to find your gaps on that topic.
  3. Work through two or three exam-style questions with hints only.
  4. Finish by asking for a five-question quiz with no hints, and write down anything you missed.
  5. Review those missed points two days later.

Thirty minutes of this beats two hours of re-reading. To schedule it across all your subjects, use an AI study plan.

Frequently asked questions

Is ChatGPT study mode free?

Yes. Study mode is included on every ChatGPT plan, including the free one, at no extra charge.

How do I turn on study mode in ChatGPT?

Open a chat, then choose Study from the plus menu or type @study. You can also go to chatgpt.com/studymode.

Is using ChatGPT study mode cheating?

Using it to understand a topic and practise is not cheating. Submitting AI-written work as your own usually is. Follow your school's rules on AI use.
