Standards

Editorial policy

How guides are researched, checked and corrected.

Our standards

  • Sources first. Facts about prices, free trials, certificates and course rules come from the official page of the company or university. Each guide lists its sources and links them where the claim is made.
  • Dated. Every guide shows when it was published and when it was last updated. We only change the updated date when the content really changes.
  • Clear about cost. We separate “free to learn”, “free badge”, “free certificate” and “paid”. If we could not confirm whether something is free, we say so or leave it out.
  • No invented numbers. We do not make up statistics, reviews or success stories. Example numbers are labelled as examples.
  • Independent. No company pays to appear in a guide or to change what we say. If we ever use affiliate links, the guide will say so clearly.

Corrections

Found something wrong? Send the page link to hello@upskillgrid.com. We check it against the official source and update the guide if it needs fixing.

Advertising

UpskillGrid may show ads, for example through Google AdSense, to keep the guides free. Ads are kept separate from guide content and never decide what we recommend.
