Free Data Analytics Courses With Certificates: What Is Really Free in 2026

By Syed Shahmikh Ali · Published 19 August 2026 · Updated 11 November 2026

The best free data analytics courses that include a certificate are Kaggle Learn's short courses, Cisco Networking Academy's Data Analytics Essentials and freeCodeCamp's Data Analysis with Python. All three are free from start to finish. The well-known Google Data Analytics Certificate is not free by default: it runs on Coursera, where you need a subscription or approved financial aid to earn the certificate.

Key takeaways

  • Kaggle Learn gives a free certificate for each short course, including Python, pandas, SQL and data visualisation.
  • Cisco's Data Analytics Essentials is a free 30-hour beginner course with a certificate.
  • Google's and IBM's data certificates on Coursera need payment or financial aid.
  • Employers care most about SQL, spreadsheets and a project that uses real data.

As AutoApplyMax's guide to free data certifications puts it, half the search results for a free data analytics certification are free courses that charge for the actual certificate. The table above separates the two.

What do you learn on Kaggle Learn?

Kaggle is a data science community owned by Google. Its Learn section has short, practical courses that run in your browser, where you write code in notebooks from the first lesson. The AutoApplyMax guide lists Python, Pandas, Intro to SQL, Advanced SQL, Data Visualization, Data Cleaning and Intro to Machine Learning among them, each taking a few hours and each giving a free certificate of completion.

These certificates are small, so group them on one line of your CV. Their real value is speed: in a weekend you can learn enough pandas to open a spreadsheet of real data and answer questions about it. Kaggle also hosts thousands of free datasets to practise on.

What does Cisco's Data Analytics Essentials cover?

Skillset Master's review of free data courses describes it as a 30-hour beginner course on Cisco's Networking Academy platform covering data types, data storytelling, visualisation and basic statistics, with the certificate included at no cost.

It suits complete beginners because it starts with spreadsheets and ideas, not code. If you have never worked with data before, take this first, then move to Kaggle for Python and SQL.

What about the Google and IBM data certificates?

They are good, well-known programmes, but they are hosted on Coursera. Analytics Vidhya's roundup makes the same point: most such courses are free to learn from in part, but may need financial aid or a trial to get the certificate.

Coursera no longer lets you audit whole courses, so the realistic free route is financial aid, one course at a time. We explain the steps in how to get Coursera courses for free.

What is a good 12-week free study plan?

  1. Weeks 1 to 3: Cisco Data Analytics Essentials. Learn what analysts do and get comfortable with spreadsheets and charts.
  2. Weeks 4 and 5: Kaggle's Intro to SQL and Advanced SQL. SQL is the skill most analyst job adverts ask for.
  3. Weeks 6 to 8: Python basics, then Kaggle's Pandas course. If Python is new, see free Python courses with certificates.
  4. Week 9: Kaggle's Data Visualization and Data Cleaning courses.
  5. Weeks 10 to 12: one project. Pick a dataset you care about, such as sports results or exam statistics, ask three questions, and answer them with charts and a short written summary.

Publish the project as a Kaggle notebook or on GitHub. A single clear project shows more than a list of certificates.

Can you get a data analyst job with free certificates?

Free certificates help you learn and show commitment, but employers hire on skills. For entry-level roles that means SQL, spreadsheets, a visualisation tool such as Power BI or Tableau, and the ability to explain what the numbers mean.

If you later want a recognised exam, Microsoft's Power BI Data Analyst certification is the usual next step, and its training is free; see free Microsoft courses and certificates.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Google Data Analytics Certificate free?

Not by default. It is on Coursera, which needs a subscription after a free trial. You can apply for Coursera financial aid to earn it without paying.

Are Kaggle certificates free?

Yes. Every Kaggle Learn course is free and gives a free certificate of completion when you finish the exercises.

Do I need to know coding to start data analytics?

No. You can begin with spreadsheets and charts. SQL and Python come next, and both can be learned for free.
